About Me

I am a multimedia “jack of all trades” living in Ottawa, Ontario, Canada with my wife and our hilariously low-to-the-ground Corgi. I play hockey, homebrew beer and watch a lot of YouTube. I consider myself a laid-back, creative, and honest personality. Sound like we might work well together? Get in touch!

 

I can handle live-film and VFX projects, but my real passion lies in motion graphics and animation. I enjoy trying new styles and combining different aesthetics to create a truly unique product. Having worked in e-learning development, I find that motion graphics and animation are one of the best ways to make information accessible and engaging.

 

Whether it’s graphic design, concept art, 3D modelling, or storyboard creation, I can do any design work you might need. Years of working in a client-oriented field have honed my ability to listen to what you’re envisioning, and translate that idea into the product you need.

 

What sets me apart from your average designer is the multimedia and development abilities I have. Looking for a new web page design, or gamification of your products? I work in HTML, CSS, JavaScript and ActionScript and can turn your design ideas into an interactive application.
