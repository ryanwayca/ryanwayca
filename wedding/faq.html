Frequently Asked Questions

Find all your answers here!

How/when do I RSVP?

You can RSVP on this very website--just look at the menu across the top of the screen and click "RSVP" - then input your information! Please send us your acceptances or regrets by April 20, 2017 at the very latest.

What will the weather be like?

It will most likely be warm, and slightly breezy/cooler in the evenings as the Opinicon is right on the lake. The ceremony will take place outside, so please be prepared for warm weather, and don't forget sunscreen!


Hopefully there will be no rain, but it's always best to be prepared. If there is rain, we may move the ceremony indoors. We recomend checking the weather before packing and dressing for the weekend events.

Where should I stay?

We really hope you'll be staying on site at The Opinicon with us for the entire weekend! Beautiful and comfortable cottage accommodations are available, and we'd love to spend that extra time with you on Friday and Sunday.


The Opinicon is approximately 30 minutes from Smiths Falls, 60 minutes from Kingston and 90 minutes from Ottawa, and there are few accommodations nearby other than those on-site. If you choose not to stay on-site, please plan your travel to and from the ceremony and reception accordingly!

How does this whole cottage set-up work?

Accommodations at The Opinicon are in recently renovated cottages. Some cottages are smaller, sleeping only 2-4 people in 1 or 2 bedrooms, and some are larger to accomodate 6-10 people in 3 or 4 bedrooms and larger gathering spaces.


Once all the RSVPs are in, Alex and Ryan will do a preliminary planning of cottage sleeping arrangements - keeping couples and families together, and being mindful that some of our quieter guests may not want to be right next door to the late-night partygoers. Then, if you're sharing a room with someone, we'll reach out to ensure you are comfortable with that arrangement. If you're not sharing a room with someone other than your spouse, we'll assume you're OK with that!


The cottage accommodations are kind of tricky to arrange, so we think it's best for us to make those plans, but if you have specific concerns or requests to share a room with someone else, please reach out to us at waytobe2017@gmail.com !

Can I park at the venue?

Yes, there is parking at the Opinicon resort.

When should I get there?

The ceremony will start promptly at 3 PM. You are welcome to come to the ceremony area and get a good seat starting at 2:15 PM. We recommend you arrive by 2:40 PM at the latest in order to give you time to find a seat.


If you're staying on site with us at The Opinicon, you can check in any time after 3 PM on Friday, and then you'll already be there for the ceremony on Saturday!

Most importantly, when will we eat?

Dinner will be served at approximately 6:30 PM on Saturday, so we recommend you ensure to eat lunch before the wedding begins. The Opinicon resort has a 1950s-style lunch and ice cream counter where you will be sure to find something you like!


If you are also staying on-site, you are welcome to bring any snacks/drinks that you would like for your cottage (which is equipped with a mini-fridge).

What if I have a dietary restriction?

We are having a buffet style dinner, so hopefully everyone will be able to find a meal option that will make them happy. If you have specific concerns or allergies, please don't hesitate to email us at waytobe2017@gmail.com and we will discuss with the Opinicon chef.

Will alcohol be served?

Yes! We are bringing kegs with 2 different styles of our home-brewed beer, and plenty of red and white wine for everyone to enjoy, on us! As well as soft drinks, coffee and tea etc. of course.


If you would like spirits or mixed drinks, those will be available for purchase at the bar.

Is there a wedding hashtag?

Yes! Please use #Waytobe2017 to help share your memories from the entire weekend with us!

Can I bring a plus one?

Unfortunately there is a limit on the number of guests we can invite and cannot accommodate a "plus-one" for everyone. The persons invited are listed on your invitation envelope. If you feel we left out someone important, please let us know.

What should I wear?

It is nice to dress up for special occasions, but it's also important to be comfortable! You might want to consider the attire something like a "garden party".


As the ceremony will take place on the grass, high-heeled shoes might be better left at home!

When is the wedding?

June 10, 2017, 3 PM.

Where is the wedding?

The ceremony will be held at The Opinicon resort, outside on the grass.


The reception will also be held at The Opinicon. Dinner will take place inside the main dining room, with drinks and dancing to follow in the upstairs lounge.

Can I take pictures?

We ask that you please consider putting down the phone or digital camera and truly being present in the moment we become husband and wife. We have hired two paid photographers to be present and taking photos throughout the entire ceremony, and will gladly share them afterwards!


But outside of the ceremony time, while you are enjoying the weekend events, take as many photos as possible! Be sure to share them with us on social media using #Waytobe2017 ! You'll also want to check out our Snapchat filter (find it by swiping left and right after taking a photo in Snapchat) that can be used near The Opinicon all weekend long!

If you must change your RSVP response, please call us as soon as you are aware, so we can make adjustments with our caterer if possible.
